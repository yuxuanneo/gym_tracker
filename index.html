How progression works

🔔 Rest-over notification, even outside the app (ntfy)

Web apps can't buzz you from the background, so Iron Log asks the free ntfy service to do it: when a rest starts, it schedules a "Rest over" push for exactly when the timer ends — delivered even if you're in another app or the phone is locked. Install the free ntfy app (App Store / Play Store), subscribe to your topic there, and paste the same topic here. Pick a long random topic (e.g. ironlog-yuxuan-x7k2p9) and treat it like a password — anyone who knows it can send you notifications. Needs internet when the rest starts; stopping a timer early can't cancel an already-scheduled notification.
